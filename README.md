## 🧭 Principles

1. **Start from the problem**, not the model.
2. **Evaluate before scaling**: quality, cost and latency metrics from day one.
3. **Record decisions** (ADRs) and their trade-offs.
4. **Observability and security** are architecture requirements, not extras.
5. **Simple and evolvable** beats sophisticated and fragile.
